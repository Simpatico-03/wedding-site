Dress Code

Celebrate in Colour

"These are suggestions — please don't feel limited. You're welcome to wear whatever makes you feel your best!"

The colour theme carries through all events. There's no strict separation between the church wedding and reception — if you'd like to change between events, that's entirely your own choice.

Bride's Side

Shades of Green

From deep forest and emerald to sage, mint, and zari — all shades of green are warmly welcome. Bridesmaids will be in gowns, so feel inspired. Whether Indian ethnic wear or western, the colour palette stays the same across all events.

Sarees Lehengas Kurtas Sherwanis Gowns Evening Dresses Suits Blazer Sets
Groom's Side

Shades of Pink

From blush and powder pink to dusty rose and deep magenta — all shades of pink are welcome. Sherwanis, kurtas, lehengas, gowns, sarees — Indian ethnic or western, the colour theme stays the same throughout all events.

Where to Shop

Find Your Outfit

Whether you're shopping from India, Dubai, or the USA, here are some trusted places to find or stitch your perfect look.

India

  • Kalyan Silks (Kottayam / Kochi)Silks, sarees, and Kerala textiles — perfect for the occasion
  • FabIndiaHandcrafted ethnic wear in natural fabrics — great for kurtas and dupattas
  • Malabar Gold & DiamondsAccessories and jewellery to complete your look
  • Local tailors, PandalamFor custom stitching — any local tailor can match fabric to the colour theme

Dubai

  • Meena Bazaar, Bur DubaiExtensive range of lehengas, sarees, and salwars in every colour
  • Karama MarketBudget-friendly ethnic wear and fabric for custom tailoring
  • Ritu Kumar / W (Mall of Emirates)Contemporary Indian fashion — great for gowns with an ethnic touch
  • Dragon Mart TailorsAffordable custom stitching with a wide fabric selection

USA

  • Utsav Fashion (online)Wide range of Indian ethnic wear shipped to the USA
  • Soch / Biba (online)Contemporary Indian wear, easy international shipping
  • BHLDN / AnthropologieFor western-style gowns and evening dresses in pastel shades
  • Local Indian boutiquesMost major US cities have Indian garment districts — check local listings

These are starting points — feel free to shop wherever you love. The colour and the joy you bring is what matters most.
