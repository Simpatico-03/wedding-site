Holy Matrimony

Venue Salem Mar Thoma Church
Time 2:30 PM

The Mar Thoma wedding is a sacred two-part sacrament blending Kerala Syrian traditions with biblical symbolism. It features the blessing of rings and a crowning ceremony, signifying a divine union. Key rituals include tying the Minnu and draping the Manthrakodi, representing lifelong togetherness and the couple's status as a new 'royal' household.
